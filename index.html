The planet's biosphere, atmosphere, hydrosphere, and geosphere work together to create conditions that support life. My body is the first instance of autopoiesis that I spot, it’s preparing itself every second, from injuries that are microscopic. Making the Connection: Applying the Academic Method to Biodesign

A logbook would comprise of 4 sections: planning, experiment, data analysis and conclusion.

Planning
The context and information for the experiment alongside a hypothesis. I would also plan out my experiment in this section, I would collate all the relevant studies and use them as a base to create a mythology. I would also write what my outcomes should be, and highlight steps that might have high points of error from any errors I may have identifies in the studies.

Experiment
I would note every weight and measurement used, even if it's different from the plan. Note any changes I notice such as changes in colour, texture etc as well as any thoughts that cross my mind whilst conducting the experiment

Data analysis
This is where I go my calculations from the data obtained during the experiment. I would map these out in and plot graphs to visually understand any possible trends

Conclusion
Relating the data analysis to the initial hypothesis and discussing if the hypothesis was correct. If the data isn't as expected this is where I can explore why it did go as expected, maybe human error or other circumstance. I would also discuss how I would improve this, assuming the experiment can be iterated.
